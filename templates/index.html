Stablecoin Supply

Total value of all dollar-pegged coins (USDT, USDC, DAI…). Rising = new cash parked inside crypto waiting to buy — historically bullish. Falling = money leaving crypto for banks.

BTC Dominance Bitcoin's share of the total crypto market

Rising dominance = money favoring BTC over altcoins (typical in fear); falling = altcoins outperforming (typical in greed). Free APIs don't offer this as history, so the app records one reading per day starting 2026-07-05 — the chart grows over time.
